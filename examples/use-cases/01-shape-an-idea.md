# Choose a first version

A volunteer organizer wants fewer empty seats at workshops. “Build a community platform” leaves the first release wide open. A useful starting point is the organizer’s last event. Twelve seats were available, people registered by email, and the organizer lost track of who still wanted a place. Treat those numbers as an example. Bring your own recent event, the signup sheet you use now, and the decision that costs someone time.

**In the planning chat**, attach the ChatGPT-only pack or the ChatGPT + Codex pack. Remove personal details from any sample sheet.

```text
notchlore. Read START.md. Help a volunteer running a 12-seat workshop manage signups. Give me one first version and a concrete example we can use to decide whether it works.
```

Ask the planner to walk through what the organizer and the thirteenth applicant would see. Suppose the organizer chooses a waiting list and wants to confirm promotions manually. Write down who can give away a seat and when the next person moves up. That decision is enough to sketch the main screen and write an acceptance example. Accounts, calendars and payment can wait for a reason to include them.

**What to keep.** A short project document with the organizer’s goal, the seat rule and one worked journey. Read it back to the organizer using twelve occupied seats, one cancellation and one person waiting. If you still cannot tell who gets the seat or who must act, settle that question before preparing a coding task.
