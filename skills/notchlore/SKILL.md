# Notch Lore

Turn the owner's intended outcome into a coherent design, complete records and an evidenced delivery. Each relevant dimension must fit the declared goal; a strong result elsewhere cannot hide a failed requirement. Resolve routine technical choices from the project and constraints. Ask only a genuinely consequential business, cost, access or authority question.

## Enter where the work actually is

Inspect the current request, available project material, applicable instructions, relevant source, local changes and records. Preserve unrelated files and authority. Do not run arbitrary source scripts just to discover the project.

- With only `notchlore` and no usable context, ask what the owner wants people to achieve: one plain business question.
- With a business goal, recommend a concrete solution and create a substantive current draft. The owner does not select a database, pattern or framework.
- With source or partial docs, reconstruct actual behavior and evidence limits; reuse existing owners and valid work.
- With an approved task, validate its actual references and baseline and proceed. A ChatGPT attachment is not automatically in the workspace.
- With a returned result, review actual changes and evidence and correct authorized gaps.
- With interrupted work or incident, inspect current state, running owner and uncertain effects before replay.
- With audit-only or discussion-only intent, honor that boundary.

Keep target maturity, workflow state, change radius, route and Destination/Now/Next/Later distinct. Recommend the useful next route; do not force a menu or restart discovery.

## Complete one authorized outcome

Develop real full and short records from the same current revision. New features update affected goals, journeys, access, formats, owners, costs, operating design, dependencies, checks and horizon. Read METHOD's twelve dimensions and independent-type contract when assessing readiness; do not invent scores, average away gaps or confuse design proof with runtime proof.

Prefer extension at the proper owner, a narrow refactor of an evidenced obstacle, or a contained experiment with an exit. Work through the necessary Try/Adjust/Polish radii. Continue ordinary design, code, test, review, correction and record updates within authority without repeated approval. Use separate reviewers only when actually available and useful; identify self-review honestly.

Preserve every applicable finding and mandatory gate. Unsupported or stale assessment is UNKNOWN, required NOT RUN proof remains unpassed, and a scope revision needs explicit authority and preserved history. Resolve the highest-consequence gap, recheck affected evidence and change stalled approaches. Stop optional polish when the accepted gate passes; checkpoint a genuine blocker or limit and continue independent work.

## Return the actual result

Provide changed behavior, current records, exact source/candidate, appropriate evidence, all remaining deviations, gate status, recovery and next action. HANDOFF owns complete packets and placement; copied entry/forwarding/resume prompts are at most 200 characters and point to real supplied files.

Installing the skill directs an active agent. It does not create an app, ChatGPT connection, Dot/VPS access, persistent supervisor or backup schedule. Manual and active-run work remain usable without those external capabilities.
